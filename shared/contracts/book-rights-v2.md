# Bookstr rights contract v2

The v2 rights record keeps the legacy status/license fields and adds
jurisdiction, evidence, attribution, and a conservative redistribution policy.
`public_seed_allowed` is true only for public-domain or open-license material
whose redistribution policy is explicitly `allowed`. Unknown, noncommercial,
and forbidden records can remain searchable or be transferred privately, but
are never eligible for default public seeding.

Source adapters must preserve evidence URLs and must not infer permission from
age, language, or a catalogue title alone. Per-work Wikisource templates and
source-specific terms take precedence over a provider-wide default.
